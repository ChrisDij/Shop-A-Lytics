# BoschCard synthetic dataset: data dictionary

All data is fictional. Generated by `generate_boschcard_data.py` (seed 2026; defaults 60,000 transactions, 5,000 students, 78 vendors, 3 Feb 2025 to 27 Sep 2026).

## Tables (per project ERD)
| Table | Notes |
|---|---|
| student | Fictional names, 8-digit student numbers, `@student.example.ac.za` emails, phone numbers with "555" in the middle. Direct identifiers exist because the ERD requires them, so the product must never expose them. |
| vendor_type | 8 niches: Restaurant, Nightclub & Bar, Transport, Pharmacy, Retail, Cinema & Theatre, Gym & Fitness*, Cafe & Coffee Shop* (*placeholders for the two unconfirmed niches) |
| vendor | 78 fictional businesses on real Stellenbosch streets. `gps` is `(longitude,latitude)`, approximate. |
| transaction | See accounting conventions below. |

## Accounting conventions
- `value` is the gross amount paid before discount, in ZAR, VAT (15%) included, 2 decimal places. VAT, tips, payment method and line items are not separate columns (ERD limit).
- `discount` is a rand amount, never greater than `abs(value)`. It follows each vendor's policy: fixed percentage, or off-peak/peak rates (restaurants, nightclubs, cinemas). Only discountable lines are discounted: cinema snacks and club cover charges are not, and pharmacy dispensing is never discounted. About 9% of rows carry no discount.
- Prices sit on real price points (for example 94.90 or 95.00) and rise 6% from 1 Mar 2026.
- Refunds and reversals (about 0.6% of rows, retail and pharmacy) are negative `value` and `discount` rows for the same student and vendor, 1 to 14 days after the original.
- Gym memberships are recurring monthly debit orders on a fixed day, stamped in the early morning, so they fall outside trading hours.
- Transport fares are base plus per-km, with late-night and peak surge. Shuttles are fixed fares and scooters are unlock plus per-minute.

## Seeded features for demonstrating the required safeguards
- **Correlation vs causation:** `ground_truth_promotions.csv` (not part of the ERD) lists 24 injected promotions, three per niche. The first promo in each niche starts in a seasonal peak (`start_in_seasonal_peak = True`), so a naive before/after comparison overstates its effect.
- **Small-cell suppression:** the last vendor in each niche is deliberately tiny (as low as 27 transactions), so minimum-count rules can be demonstrated.
- **Re-identification risk:** heavy-tailed student activity (a few very frequent students) and rare gender/age/residence combinations.
- **Competitor sensitivity:** vendors of one niche are clustered on the same streets, so competitor comparisons and peer benchmarks can be tested.
- **Seasonality:** exams, winter recess, summer break, start-of-year peak, month-end payday effect, Black Friday (retail) and flu season (pharmacy).
